                              INITIATIVE IL26-001




 1       AN ACT Relating to the Washington parents' bill        of   rights;
 2   amending RCW 28A.605.005; and creating new sections.


 3   BE IT ENACTED BY THE PEOPLE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:


 4       Sec. 1. RCW 28A.605.005 and 2025 c 369 s 501 are each amended to
 5   read as follows:
 6       (1) Subsections 2 through 5 of this section reinstate Initiative
 7   2081's laws guaranteeing parents' rights, including reviewing their
 8   children's instructional materials, accessing their school records,
 9   being notified when medical services are provided, and opting-out-of
10   certain classes. Voter approval of this initiative provides two years
11   of constitutional protection from legislative interference, which
12   reinforces Initiative 2081's laws guaranteeing parents' rights.
13       (2) The people find that: (a) Parents are the primary
14   stakeholders in their children's upbringing; (b) parental involvement
15   is a significant factor in increasing student achievement; and (c)
16   access   to   student   information    encourages   greater   parental
17   involvement.
18       (3) Parents and legal guardians of public school children younger
19   than 18 years old have all of the following rights:
20       (a) To examine the textbooks, curriculum, and supplemental
21   material used in their child's classroom;

 1       (b)(i) To inspect their child's public school records in
 2   accordance with RCW 28A.605.030, and to receive a copy of their
 3   child's records within 10 business days of submitting a written
 4   request, either electronically or on paper.
 5       (ii) Parents or legal guardians must not be required to appear in
 6   person for the purposes of requesting or validating a request for
 7   their child's public school records.
 8       (iii) No charge may be imposed on a parent or legal guardian to
 9   receive such records electronically. Any charges for a paper copy of
10   such records must be reasonable and set forth in the official
11   policies and procedures of the school district.
12       (iv) Public school records include all of the following:
13       (A) Academic records including, but not limited to, test and
14   assessment scores in accordance with RCW 28A.230.195;
15       (B) Medical or health records;
16       (C) Records of any mental health counseling;
17       (D) Records of any vocational counseling;
18       (E) Records of discipline, including expulsions and suspensions
19   under RCW 28A.600.015;
20       (F) Records of attendance, including unexcused absences in
21   accordance with RCW 28A.225.020;
22       (G) Records associated with a child's screening for learning
23   challenges, exceptionalities, plans for an individualized education
24   program, or plan adopted under section 504 of the rehabilitation act
25   of 1973; and
26       (H) Any other student-specific files, documents, or other
27   materials that are maintained by the public school;
28       (c) To receive prior notification when medical services are being
29   offered to their child, except where emergency medical treatment is
30   required. In cases where emergency medical treatment is required, the
31   parent and legal guardian must be notified as soon as practicable
32   after the treatment is rendered;
33       (d) To receive notification when any medical service or
34   medications have been provided to their child that could result in
35   any financial impact to the parent's or legal guardian's health
36   insurance payments or copays;
37       (e) To receive notification when the school has arranged directly
38   or indirectly for medical treatment that results in follow-up care
39   beyond normal school hours. Follow-up care includes monitoring the

 5       (g) To receive immediate notification if law enforcement
 6   personnel question their child, except in cases where the parent or
 7   legal guardian has been accused of abusing or neglecting the child;

32   no later than 30 days prior to the beginning of the school year, and
33   to be notified in writing as soon as feasible of any revisions to
34   such calendar. Such calendar must be posted to the public school's
35   website and must include, at a minimum, student attendance days and
36   any event that requires parent or student attendance outside of

15   shall not be required to release any records or information regarding
16   a student's medical or health records or mental health counseling
17   records to a parent during the pendency of an investigation of child
18   abuse or neglect conducted by any law enforcement agency or the
19   department of children, youth, and families where the parent is the
20   target of the investigation, unless the parent has obtained a court
21   order.

26   is a significant factor in increasing student achievement; and (c)
27   access   to   student   information    encourages   greater   parental
28   involvement.
29       (2) Parents and legal guardians of children enrolled in public
30   schools as defined in RCW 28A.150.010 have the following rights:
31       (a) To access their child's classroom and school-sponsored
32   activities to observe in accordance with RCW 28A.605.020 and to
33   examine the curriculum, textbooks, instructional materials, and
34   supplemental instructional materials used in their child's classroom
35   in accordance with policies and procedures;
36       (b) To inspect and review their child's education records and to
37   request and receive a copy of their child's education records within
38   a reasonable period of time, but not more than 45 days, of submitting
39   a request in accordance with the federal family educational rights

 1   and privacy act of 1974, Title 20 U.S.C. Sec. 1232g, as in effect on
 2   January 1, 2025, and RCW 28A.605.030.
 3       (ii) Parents and legal guardians choosing to inspect and review
 4   their child's education records may not be required by a public
 5   school to appear in person for the purposes of requesting or
 6   validating a request for their child's education records, provided
 7   the public school can ascertain the identity of the requestor.
 8       (iii) No charge may be imposed on a parent or legal guardian to
 9   inspect or review their child's education records or for the costs of
10   searching for or retrieving the education records. Any charges for a
11   copy of such records must be reasonable, not prevent a parent, legal
12   guardian, or eligible child from exercising the right to inspect and
13   review the child's education records, and be set forth in the
14   official policies and procedures of the school district and public
15   school.
16       (iv) Education records means those official records, files, and
17   data directly related to a student and maintained by the public
18   school including, but not limited to, records encompassing all the
19   material kept in the child's cumulative folder, such as general
20   identifying data, records of attendance and of academic work
21   completed, records of achievement and results of evaluative tests,
22   disciplinary status, test protocols, and individualized education
23   programs;
24       (v) Education records do not include records that are kept in the
25   sole possession of the maker, are used only as a personal memory aid,
26   and are not accessible or revealed to any other person except a
27   temporary substitute for the maker of the record;
28       (vi) Nothing in this section changes the access and disclosure
29   provisions established in chapter 70.02 RCW related to health care
30   information;
31       (c) To receive notification upon receipt of a report that a
32   criminal action is alleged to have been committed against their child
33   on school property during the school ay or during a school sponsored
34   activity including immediate notification if there has been a
35   shooting on school property, or their child has been detained based
36   on probably cause of involvement in criminal activity on school
37   property during the school day;
38       (d) To receive immediate notification upon receipt of a report
39   that their child is alleged to be the victim, target, or recipient of
