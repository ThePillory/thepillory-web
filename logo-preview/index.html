Logo concepts

Starting points, not finished logos. Each one is shown on the page background, reversed on navy, and at small icon sizes. Pick any that feel right, or mix pieces from several.

The Pillory

Today's wordmark, with slightly tighter spacing. Quiet and trustworthy, and nothing new to learn. Pairs with monogram 4 or 5 for the app icon.

2 · Wordmark

THEPillory
THEPillory

A small, spaced-out "THE" over "Pillory", like a newspaper nameplate. More compact, so it fits square spaces better.

3 · Wordmark

The Pllory
The Pllory

The "i" becomes a small column, a quiet nod to the pillar in Pillory. Playful. At very small sizes the column can blur into an "l", so it needs a plain version for icons.
