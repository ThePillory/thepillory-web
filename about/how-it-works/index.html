How it works

How residents' reports will become a public, evidence-first record.

Live now: your officials and their votes, bills mapped to the Constitution, and county meeting agendas. Opens when accounts launch: verification, reports and corroboration (steps 1 to 6 below).

  1. 1

    Join and verify

    Participants verify uniqueness and residency. Real people, one voice each. Identities are protected by default.

  2. 2

    Make a claim

    Claims are tied to a place, category, and timeframe. No vague accusations or drive-by posts.

  3. 3

    Attach evidence

    Documents, links, photos, records. Claims without evidence don't travel far.

  4. 4

    Community actions

    Others can corroborate with evidence, support with context, or identify as affected, all without public pile-ons.

  5. 5

    Aggregate signal

    Individual inputs stay private while patterns emerge: consistency, volume, quality, confidence.

  6. 6

    Publish accountability

    When thresholds are met, the output becomes a structured, shareable record for media, officials, or institutions.
