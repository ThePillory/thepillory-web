Sing Tao News Corporation Ltd.:  Sing Tao News Corporation Ltd.: The STUS entities are privately-owned, Chinese
language media companies, which create and distribute periodicals, newspapers, broadcasts, Internet materials
and other news-related content with the goal of providing the Chinese-speaking community with engaging, full,
and frank discussion of significant and newsworthy events. As part of their commercial news operations, the
entities create news reporting, opinion pieces, and other commentary regarding contemporary issues. The ultimate
parent company of the entities, Sing Tao News Corporation Ltd., is a publicly traded, for-profit corporation,
which is not owned by or affiliated with any foreign government or political party. To this extent, the STUS
entities believe that they are similarly situated to other for-profit media companies operating in the United
States. The Department of Justice's FARA Unit concluded this constitutes political activity for purposes of
FARA.
